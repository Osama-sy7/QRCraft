Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At our Web Tools Suite, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our tools operate entirely client-side inside your browser. We do not require users to create an account, provide personal details, or upload sensitive media to external database servers. All processing (such as image compression, text counting, and QR generation) happens locally on your device.

2. Log Files & Analytics

We follow a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Google DoubleClick Cookie (AdSense & Advertising)

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

4. Third Party Advertising Partners

Some of advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include Google AdSense and third-party ad networks. Third-party ad servers use technology to display advertisements directly to your browser.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We respect your privacy rights. Users located in the European Union (EU) or California (US) have full rights to request deletion of data or opt-out of personalized tracking cookies at any time via their browser preferences.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by visiting our home page.
